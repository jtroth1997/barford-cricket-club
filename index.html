More than the score

A proper friendly
cricket club.

Barford Cricket Club has been part of village life for generations. Today we play friendly Sunday fixtures and midweek cricket on a grass wicket at Snitterfield.

There is no pressure to play every week. We welcome different ages and abilities, can provide kit when needed, and enjoy a full social calendar — including our annual tour.
